Feel free to reach out to us for components i.e. mainboard as some are easier to purchase in higher volume.

2: 3D printing the files

  • Case, Backplate, mounting brackets:
    • Material: PLA+, ABS, Greentec Pro
    • Layer-height: 0.12
    • Walls: 4
    • Infill: 15%
    • Draw a fuzzy Skin on the outside with Settings:
      • Generator Mode: Displacement
      • noise type: Classic
      • point distance: 0.35
      • thickness: 0.08
    • example 3MF file
  • Buttons:
    • Material: TPU
    • Layer-height: 0.20
    • example 3MF file

8: initial firmware flashing and first power up

  • connect your EPV to a USB-C power source and give it a minute to initialize the battery management and charge the battery
  • create your config.txt and upload to the SD card along with AGL data to your flying region
    • config-builder-here
  • enter the bootsel mode by pressing volume-up and power on for 4 seconds
  • find a USB mass storage device called RPI-RP2
  • copy an EPV firmware.uf2 file to the USB device download-here
  • wait until the EPV reboots and splash screen shows up
  • initialize the sensors how-to

9: Basic tests

  • can the volume be controlled with the side buttons
  • is the G-Force display ~1.0 with no motion?
  • blow gently into an opening to create a pressure difference -> climb rate should change and vario should beep
  • are you getting any SAT lock when outside?
  • once altitude changes to AMSL, with more than 8 sats, you can even test flight detection with a little ride on your bike :)

assembly tips

  • the GPS antenna is the metal/silver plate on top of the brown ceramic plate. Its tuned by those scratches you might see - which is absolutely normal. Put some thin tape (aka Tesa) on top of that to "protect" the tune and surface.
  • use a lighter and some gentle heat on areas of the printed parts, where the surface appears brighter / matte.
  • use a powerdrill to screw in a dummy screw quickly and let it rest for a minute. The friction will melt the plastic and create a nice thread around the screw.
