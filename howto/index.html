How do I update my Vario for new features?

  • when turned off, press and hold the power-button and volume-up-button for 5 seconds to enter flashing/bootsel mode.
  • a USB-drive/mass storage device does now appear when plugged into a computer or phone
  • copy your firmware.uf2 file to the RPI-RP2 drive
  • the vario boots with the new firmware once completed
  • unplug the vario

How do I get a recorded flight / IGC-file?

Your flight records are stored on the SD card and currently there is no access through the USB of the vario possible.

  • turn the vario off and eject the SD card
  • use a cardreader on your mobile or PC to access the card files

How to get AGL data shown?

  • turn the vario off and eject the SD card
  • use a cardreader on your mobile or PC to access the card files
  • go to the map here and download each tile you are flying at (one or multiple)
  • extract each content (files like N46E010-019.HGT) to your SD card root (not a subfolder)
  • insert SD card and turn on the vario
  • wait for your SAT lock and observe your mAGL value
  • done

How do I end an ongoing flight recoding immediately?

  • to end a flight, like flight timer is running, you can press and hold both volume buttons for 1 second
  • the flight mode will switch to "landed"
  • IGC recording will stop gracefully
  • post flight stats screen will be displayed
  • if you are still flying (or still above 2m/s) a new flight will be detected automatically

How do I change my configuration?

  • turn the vario off and eject the SD card
  • use a cardreader on your mobile or PC to access the card files
  • use our configurator here
  • fill out the form and hit download to retrieve your config.txt
  • save the config.txt to your SD card and insert into your vario
  • done

How do I force-reset my vario?

If your vario stuck in any unexpected case like a filesystem issue, a bug or elsewhat you can power down your vario like so:

  • press and hold the powerbutton and volume-UP button for 15 seconds
  • the device will be force-powered-down from any state
  • done, turn the vario on normally
